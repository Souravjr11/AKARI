伝統の物語 • Heritage Story

The Story of AKARI (あかり)

Founded with a profound passion for authentic washoku — the traditional culinary philosophy recognized by UNESCO as intangible cultural heritage — AKARI brings true Japanese craftsmanship to Thakurpukur, Kolkata.

Our founder and executive chef trained for 15 years in historic Kyoto ryotei kitchens before bringing that uncompromising craftsmanship to Kolkata, where everyone can savor authentic Japanese culinary elegance.

Our Core Promises:

  • Daily inspection of fresh seafood and prime ingredients.
  • 18-hour authentic simmering of natural pork & chicken tonkotsu stocks with no artificial additives.
  • Seasoned sushi rice prepared with traditional Japanese dashi vinegar.
